The Stewardship of Grace

At Broken Barriers Church, we believe that the work of God is fueled by the people of God. We recognize that in many modern settings, the topic of money has become a source of "religious noise" and manipulation. We desire to remove those barriers and return to the Original Design: a community of faithful members who give out of a transformed heart, not under the pressure of a legalistic mandate.

Grace-Bound Giving

In the New Testament, the church is not governed by a national tax, but by Grace. The New Testament pattern is simple and powerful:

  • Proportionate: Giving as God has prospered you (1 Corinthians 16:2)
  • Purposed: Deciding in your heart what to give (2 Corinthians 9:7)
  • Cheerful: Giving because you love the Lord and want to see His Kingdom restored

Guarding Against "Ministerial Drift"

We acknowledge a dangerous trend in modern ministry: as ministerial budgets increase, there is often a temptation for preachers to elevate tithing to a "spiritual requirement" or a "key to blessing." At Broken Barriers, we refuse to "bind heavy burdens" on you. We choose financial responsibility over lavish spending.

Our Promise to You

Your giving is not a "payment" for services; it is a partnership in the Gospel. We promise to be transparent with our finances and to use every dollar with wisdom to accomplish the Great Commission. We do not want your money out of pressure; we want your heart to be aligned with Christ.
