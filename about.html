Our Story

How Broken Barriers Church Began

Broken Barriers Church was born out of a deep conviction that the modern church had strayed from its original design. We saw too many believers burdened by man-made traditions, religious complexity, and institutional barriers that obscured the simple, powerful Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Our founding vision was clear: to tear down every wall standing between people and Jesus Christ. The original barrier—sin—was demolished by Christ's sacrifice. We refuse to allow new barriers to be erected in its place.

Our Mission

At Broken Barriers Church, we are committed to restoring the Kingdom exactly as He designed it. This means:

  • Returning to biblical authority and truth
  • Removing religious noise and complexity
  • Fostering genuine worship and authentic community
  • Equipping believers to live out their faith boldly
  • Reaching the lost with the uncompromised Gospel

What Makes Us Different

We are not interested in building an empire or following the latest church growth trends. We are passionate about faithfulness over fame, substance over size, and truth over trends. We believe the church should be marked by biblical teaching, reverent worship, authentic fellowship, and missional living.

Our Commitment

We are committed to being a church that honors God, upholds His Word, loves His people, and proclaims His Gospel without compromise. We welcome all who are seeking Jesus Christ and are willing to follow Him on His terms, not ours.
