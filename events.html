Connecting Through Community

At Broken Barriers Church, we believe that life transformation happens in community. Join us for regular gatherings, special events, and opportunities to grow in faith together.

Special Events

Throughout the year, we host special events designed to deepen our faith, strengthen our community, and reach our neighbors with the Gospel.

  • Easter Celebration - Celebrating the resurrection of Jesus Christ
