Prayer & Connection

The Power of Prayer

"The prayer of a righteous person has great power as it is working." - James 5:16

At Broken Barriers Church, we believe prayer is not just a religious activity—it's vital communication with our living God who hears and answers. We are a praying church because we serve a prayer-hearing God.

How We Pray Together

Sunday Morning Prayer

Every Sunday, we dedicate time in our service to pray together as a church family—for our needs, our community, and the world.

Prayer Meetings

Join us for dedicated times of corporate prayer where we intercede for our church, nation, and the advancement of God's Kingdom.

Personal Prayer Requests

Share your needs with our church family. We believe in bearing one another's burdens through prayer and support.

Pastoral Prayer

Our pastoral team is available to pray with you personally for specific needs, decisions, or spiritual concerns.

Submit a Prayer Request

We would be honored to pray for you. Whether you're facing a crisis, celebrating a blessing, or seeking God's guidance, we want to stand with you in prayer.

Need Prayer?

Contact us to share your prayer request

Send Prayer Request

What We Pray For

  • Salvation - For those who don't yet know Jesus Christ
  • Spiritual Growth - For believers to mature in faith and holiness
  • Church Unity - For love, harmony, and biblical unity in our body
  • Faithful Leadership - For wisdom and integrity in our leaders
  • Gospel Mission - For boldness and fruitfulness in evangelism
  • Personal Needs - For healing, provision, guidance, and comfort
  • Cultural Faithfulness - For the church to stand firm in truth with grace

Small Groups & Fellowship

Life transformation happens best in community. Our small groups provide opportunities for deeper relationships, biblical study, accountability, and mutual encouragement.

Why Small Groups Matter

Deeper Relationships

In a smaller setting, you can build authentic friendships and meaningful connections with fellow believers.

Biblical Community

Experience the "one another" commands of Scripture—encouraging, serving, praying for, and loving one another.

Spiritual Growth

Grow in your understanding of God's Word through discussion, questions, and mutual learning.

Accountability

Walk in integrity with brothers and sisters who know you, encourage you, and lovingly hold you accountable.

We'd love to help you find a group that's the right fit for you. Contact us to learn more about current groups and connection opportunities.
