Sermons & Teaching

Biblical Preaching for Today

At Broken Barriers Church, we believe the Word of God is living, active, and sufficient for all of life. Our preaching is expositional, meaning we work through books of the Bible verse-by-verse, allowing Scripture to speak for itself.

Our Preaching Philosophy

We are committed to:

  • Expositional Preaching - Teaching through entire books of the Bible systematically
  • Biblical Authority - Submitting to Scripture as our final authority
  • Christ-Centered Teaching - Pointing to Jesus in all of Scripture
  • Practical Application - Showing how God's Word applies to daily life
  • Clear Communication - Making God's truth accessible to all

What to Expect from Our Preaching

Faithful to Scripture

We don't preach our opinions or follow cultural trends. We preach what God has said in His Word, even when it's uncomfortable or countercultural.

Applicable to Life

God's Word is not just ancient history—it's alive and powerful for today. We show how Scripture speaks to our real-life struggles and joys.

Clear and Understandable

We avoid unnecessary jargon and explain biblical concepts clearly so that everyone can understand and grow.

God-Glorifying

The goal of every sermon is to exalt Christ, magnify His grace, and call people to worship, obedience, and faith.
