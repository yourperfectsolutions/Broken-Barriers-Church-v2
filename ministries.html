Ministries

Serving Christ, Building His Church

"As each has received a gift, use it to serve one another, as good stewards of God's varied grace." - 1 Peter 4:10

At Broken Barriers Church, we believe every believer is called to serve. Our ministries exist to equip the saints, strengthen the body, and advance the Gospel.

Our Ministry Areas

Worship Ministry

Our worship ministry leads the congregation in God-honoring, Christ-exalting worship through music, prayer, and Scripture reading. We sing both historic hymns and modern worship songs that magnify the character of God and proclaim biblical truth.

Our Worship Philosophy

We believe worship is about God, not us. It should be reverent yet joyful, traditional yet fresh, God-centered and gospel-saturated. We value:

  • Theologically rich lyrics that teach truth
  • Corporate singing that unites the church
  • Musical excellence in service of the Word
  • Authenticity over performance

Children's Ministry

We love children and want them to know Jesus Christ from their earliest years. Our children's ministry provides age-appropriate biblical teaching in a loving, safe environment.

Our Approach

  • Family-Centered - We believe parents are the primary disciplers of their children
  • Gospel-Focused - Children hear the good news of Jesus clearly and regularly
  • Age-Appropriate - Teaching is tailored to children's developmental stages
  • Safe & Loving - We maintain high standards for child safety and care

Note: Children are always welcome in our main worship services. We encourage families to worship together.

Youth Ministry

Our youth ministry is committed to building the next generation of faithful disciples who love God's Word, live for His glory, and stand firm in truth. We provide biblical teaching, mentorship, and community for teenagers.

What We Offer

  • Regular youth gatherings for fellowship and teaching
  • Opportunities to serve and lead in the church
  • Mentorship relationships with godly adults
  • Special events and retreats
  • Preparation for adult membership and leadership

Men's Ministry

Our men's ministry equips men to be godly leaders in their homes, churches, and communities. We challenge men to live with courage, integrity, and devotion to Christ.

Our Focus

  • Biblical manhood and leadership
  • Accountability and brotherhood
  • Marriage and fatherhood
  • Work, ministry, and calling
  • Spiritual disciplines and growth

Women's Ministry

Our women's ministry encourages women to grow in godliness, serve faithfully, and mentor one another in the faith. We create opportunities for women to study God's Word together and support each other in their various callings.

Our Focus

  • Biblical womanhood and discipleship
  • Marriage, motherhood, and family
  • Spiritual growth and maturity
  • Hospitality and service
  • Mentoring relationships (Titus 2)

Outreach & Missions

We are called to be witnesses of Jesus Christ in our community and to the ends of the earth. Our outreach and missions ministry seeks to proclaim the Gospel locally and support faithful missionaries globally.

How We Engage

  • Local Evangelism - Sharing the Gospel in our community
  • Community Service - Meeting practical needs with Christ's love
  • Missionary Support - Partnering with faithful Gospel workers around the world
  • Short-Term Trips - Opportunities to serve and learn on the mission field

Get Involved

Every member of Broken Barriers Church is called to serve according to their gifts and calling. Whether you're gifted in teaching, serving, leading worship, caring for children, or hospitality, there's a place for you to use your gifts for God's glory.
