New Here? Welcome!

We're Glad You're Here

Whether you're exploring faith for the first time or looking for a new church home, we'd love to welcome you to Broken Barriers Church. We're a community of believers committed to following Jesus Christ and living out His Word together.

What to Expect on Sunday

Service Time

Sunday Morning Worship begins at 10:00 AM. We recommend arriving 10-15 minutes early to find parking, get settled, and meet some friendly faces.

Dress Code

Come as you are! We have a casual, welcoming atmosphere. Some dress up, some dress down—what matters most is that you're here.

Parking & Entrance

Parking is available in our main lot. Greeters will be at the entrance to welcome you and help you find your way.

Children

Children are always welcome in our services. We love to hear their joyful noise and want families to worship together.

Our Sunday Service

Our worship services typically include:

  • Worship Through Music - We sing hymns and modern worship songs that exalt Christ and proclaim truth
  • Prayer - We pray for our church, our community, and the world
  • Scripture Reading - We read God's Word aloud together
  • Biblical Preaching - Our pastor teaches verse-by-verse through books of the Bible
  • Communion - We observe the Lord's Supper regularly as a church family

What We Believe About Worship

We believe worship is about God, not us. We gather to glorify Him, hear from His Word, and respond with reverence, joy, and obedience. Our services are designed to be welcoming yet reverent, engaging yet God-centered.

First-Time Guest?

We'd love to meet you! After the service, feel free to introduce yourself to our pastor or one of our greeters. We don't put visitors on the spot, but we do want you to know you're welcome here.
