Our Mission

At Broken Barriers Church, our mission is to tear down every wall standing between people and Jesus Christ.

Sin was the original barrier — but the sacrifice of Jesus demolished it completely, once and for all. What we refuse to allow is for man-made traditions, institutional pride, and religious complexity to erect new walls in its place, obscuring the very Gospel that sets people free.
