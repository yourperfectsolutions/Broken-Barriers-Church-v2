Our Mission

Sin was the original barrier — but the sacrifice of Jesus demolished it completely, once and for all. What we refuse to allow is for man-made traditions, institutional pride, and religious complexity to erect new walls in its place, obscuring the very Gospel that sets people free.

At Broken Barriers Church, our mission is to tear down every wall standing between people and Jesus Christ. We are committed to removing the "religious noise" that burdens believers and returning to the Original Design that Jesus established.

We believe the church must be built on Jesus Christ, governed by the Word of God, and empowered by the Holy Spirit — simple, powerful, and transformative.

What We Believe
