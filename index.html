Our Mission

Tearing Down Every Wall

At Broken Barriers Church, our mission is to tear down every wall standing between people and Jesus Christ.

Sin was the original barrier — but the sacrifice of Jesus demolished it completely, once and for all. What we refuse to allow is for man-made traditions, institutional pride, and religious complexity to erect new walls in its place.

We are committed to restoring the Kingdom, exactly as He designed it.

Restoring Biblical Faith

We believe the church must return to its biblical foundation — simple, powerful, and transformative.

This means removing the "religious noise" that obscures the Gospel, rejecting man-made traditions that burden believers, and embracing the Original Design that Jesus established.

Biblical authority, authentic worship, genuine community, and bold mission.

"For freedom Christ has set us free"

Stand firm therefore, and do not submit again to a yoke of slavery.

— Galatians 5:1

What to Expect When You Visit

Biblical Preaching

Experience verse-by-verse teaching through books of the Bible. No gimmicks, no entertainment—just the powerful Word of God faithfully proclaimed.

Christ-Centered Worship

Join us in singing hymns and worship songs that exalt Jesus and proclaim biblical truth. Worship is about God, not performance.

Warm Community

Meet a family of believers committed to loving one another, serving together, and growing in faith. You'll find genuine fellowship here.

Come As You Are

Whether you're new to faith or a lifelong believer, you're welcome here. Dress casually, bring your questions, and encounter Jesus.
