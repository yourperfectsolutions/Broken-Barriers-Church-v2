Church Leadership

Biblical Leadership

At Broken Barriers Church, we believe Christ leads His church through biblically qualified elders who shepherd, teach, protect, and lead the congregation faithfully under the authority of Scripture. Our leadership structure is designed to honor God's Word and serve His people with humility and integrity.

Our Pastor

Lead Pastor & Teaching Elder

Our pastor serves as the primary teaching elder, responsible for preaching God's Word faithfully, shepherding the congregation, and providing spiritual leadership. He is called to equip the saints for the work of ministry and to model godly character for the body of Christ.

Elder Qualifications

Our elders are held to the biblical standard outlined in 1 Timothy 3 and Titus 1:

  • Above reproach - Living a life of integrity and moral excellence
  • Faithful in marriage and family - One-woman man, managing household well
  • Spiritually mature - Not a new convert, self-controlled, respectable
  • Able to teach - Skilled in handling Scripture and sound doctrine
  • Not quarrelsome - Gentle, patient, not quick-tempered
  • Hospitable and generous - Loving others and giving freely

Deacons

Deacons serve the practical needs of the church, allowing elders to focus on prayer, teaching, and spiritual oversight. They are men of good reputation, full of the Spirit and wisdom, who serve with dignity and devotion.

Our Commitment to You

Our leadership team is committed to:

  • Preaching and teaching God's Word faithfully
  • Shepherding the flock with love and care
  • Protecting the church from false teaching
  • Leading by example in godliness and service
  • Being available for counsel, prayer, and care
  • Equipping believers for ministry and mission

Accountability & Transparency

We believe in leading with transparency and accountability. Our elders are accountable to one another, to the congregation, and ultimately to Jesus Christ, the Head of the church. We welcome your questions, feedback, and prayers as we seek to serve you faithfully.
