What We Believe

Our Foundation

At Broken Barriers Church, we believe the church must be built on Jesus Christ, governed by the Word of God, and empowered by the Holy Spirit. We desire to honor God with a serious, gracious, and biblical faith that reflects the pattern of Christ, the apostles, and the early church.

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, sufficient, trustworthy, and final authority for what we believe and how we live. Scripture is the foundation for our doctrine, our worship, our leadership, and our life together as a church.

God

We believe there is one true and living God, eternally existing in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. God is holy, sovereign, all-wise, all-powerful, and worthy of all worship, obedience, and glory.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man, born of a virgin, sinless in life, and the only Savior of sinners. He died on the cross for our sins, rose bodily from the grave, ascended to heaven, and now reigns as Lord and Head of the church.

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit convicts of sin, gives new life, indwells believers, sanctifies God's people, and empowers faithful Christian living. We believe the Spirit always works in harmony with the Word of God and never in contradiction to it.

Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace alone through faith alone in Christ alone. All people are sinners in need of repentance and forgiveness. Those who trust in Christ are forgiven, declared righteous before God, adopted into His family, and made new by His grace.

The Church

We believe the church belongs to Jesus Christ and exists to glorify God through worship, preaching, discipleship, fellowship, prayer, and gospel mission. We believe the local church is essential to Christian growth, accountability, and service.

Church Leadership

We believe Christ leads His church through biblically qualified elders and deacons. Elders are called to shepherd, teach, protect, and lead the church faithfully under the authority of Scripture.

Baptism and the Lord's Supper

We believe Jesus gave two ordinances to His church: believer's baptism and the Lord's Supper. These are acts of obedience and visible testimonies of the gospel, though not the means of salvation.

Marriage, Sexuality, and Gender

We believe God created humanity male and female and that marriage is a covenant union between one man and one woman. We believe sexual intimacy is reserved for marriage, and we seek to uphold God's design with both truth and compassion.

Last Things

We believe Jesus Christ will personally and visibly return. We believe in the resurrection of the dead, the final judgment, eternal life for the redeemed, and eternal punishment for the unrepentant. These truths call us to live with hope, holiness, and urgency.

Our Conviction

We stand on the truths that make Christianity what it is: the holiness of God, the authority of Scripture, the gospel of Jesus Christ, the necessity of the new birth, the purity of the church, and the certainty of Christ's return. Our desire is to remain faithful, clear, and Christ-centered in all things.
